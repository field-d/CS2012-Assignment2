Hamlet Review

Reviewer: Josh Kiely

Publication: The Daily Times

Date: December 10, 2025

An absolutely stunning performance! The actors brought the story to life with such passion and energy.

From the moment the curtain rose, the audience was transported to the dark and brooding world of Elsinore Castle. The lead actor's portrayal of Hamlet was nothing short of brilliant, capturing the prince's descent into madness with remarkable subtlety and power.

The supporting cast equally impressed, with standout performances from the actors playing Ophelia and Claudius. The chemistry between the actors was palpable, making every scene feel authentic and emotionally charged.

The set design deserves special mention - the minimalist approach allowed the performances to take center stage while still creating the oppressive atmosphere of the Danish court. Lighting was used masterfully to highlight key moments and add to the overall mood.

This production of Hamlet is a must-see for theatre lovers. Cat Eyes Theatre Company has once again proven why they are one of the leading theatrical companies in the region.
