Hamlet Review

Reviewer:Brian Dylan

Publication: City Lights Magazine

Date: December 10, 2025

A must-see show! The chemistry between the cast was palpable and the storytelling was captivating.

This ensemble-driven Hamlet thrives on dynamic relationships and emotionally charged interactions.

Each character feels vividly realized, making the unfolding tragedy all the more gripping.

The production's blend of energy, clarity, and dramatic intensity keeps audiences fully engaged from the first moment to the final scene. A truly compelling theatrical experience.
