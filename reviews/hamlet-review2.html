Hamlet Review

Reviewer: Barry Lewis

Publication: Theatre Weekly

Date: December 10, 2025

A mesmerizing experience from start to finish. The set design and costumes were breathtaking.

This production of Hamlet elevates Shakespeare's tragedy with visuals that feel both timeless and strikingly modern.

The atmosphere created onstage pulls the audience deep into Elsinore's world, enhancing every emotional beat.

The performances are rich and nuanced, bringing fresh life to the iconic characters. A visually stunning triumph.
